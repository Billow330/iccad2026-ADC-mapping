\noindent\textbf{Analytical mechanism of the inversion (answers \#707A-W1, \#707C-C3/Q2, \#707D-W3).}
A uniform $b$-bit ADC with calibrated full-scale $V_{FS}$ splits its error on a MAC
output $y$ into a \emph{granular} (resolution) term and an \emph{overload/clipping}
term. With saturation rate $s=\Pr[\,|y|>V_{FS}\,]$ and step $\Delta=2V_{FS}/2^{b}$,
\begin{equation*}
D_{\mathrm{ADC}}(b)=\underbrace{D_{\mathrm{gran}}(b)}_{\approx (1-s)\,\Delta^{2}/12\;\propto\;(1-s)\,V_{FS}^{2}\,4^{-b}}+\underbrace{D_{\mathrm{ovl}}}_{\mathbb{E}[(|y|-V_{FS})^{2}\mid |y|>V_{FS}]\;\text{(independent of }b)}.
\end{equation*}
Reducing one bit thus multiplies \emph{only} the granular term by $4$ and leaves
clipping unchanged. The bit-reduction perturbation a group receives is therefore its
granular budget $\Delta D_{\mathrm{gran}}$, which then propagates to the loss along
the attention/FFN/residual paths (Fig.~5). Two measured facts (Table~S4) show why
\emph{no local statistic can rank this loss impact}. (i)~$\Delta D_{\mathrm{gran}}$
does not track saturation: since $D_{\mathrm{gran}}\!\propto\!(1{-}s)\,V_{FS}^{2}4^{-b}$,
the high-saturation attention input projection $W_{qkv}$ has the \emph{largest}
budget (large $V_{FS}$), not the smallest. (ii)~The budget is \emph{decoupled} from
the loss by propagation: across groups $\Delta D_{\mathrm{gran}}$ spans
$\approx\!740\times$, yet the resulting hidden-state drift spans only
$\approx\!1.6\times$ and is non-monotone in the measured $\Delta$PPL. Saturation
($\rho{=}{-}0.70$) and Hessian ($\rho{=}0.20$) each see only one local statistic and
miss this propagation, which is exactly why the paper measures $\Delta$PPL directly.

\vspace{0.8em}
\noindent\textbf{Table S4. Mechanism diagnostics: a verified decomposition and a
local-error/loss decoupling} (OPT-125M, INT8 per-channel weights, p99-clip, group
probe $7{\to}6$). The sensitivity ranking itself (FFN$>$attention; $W_{fc2}$ most
sensitive at the paper's operating point) is in Tables~S1--S2 and paper Table~4;
this table explains \emph{why no local proxy reproduces it}. \emph{Decomposition
law:} in every group one-bit reduction scales the granular error
$D_{\mathrm{gran}}$ by $4.06\!\approx\!4^{1}$ and the clipping error
$D_{\mathrm{ovl}}$ by $1.00$ --- the bit-width touches only the granular term.
\emph{Local error does not track saturation:} the high-saturation $W_{qkv}$ has the
\emph{largest} granular budget $\Delta D_{\mathrm{gran}}$ (large $V_{FS}$), not the
smallest. \emph{Decoupling:} $\Delta D_{\mathrm{gran}}$ spans $\approx\!740\times$
across groups, yet hidden-state drift spans only $\approx\!1.6\times$ and output KL
$\approx\!2.2\times$ --- the local ADC error is decoupled from its output (and loss)
effect by residual-stream propagation, so neither saturation, local MSE, nor even
these output diagnostics rank the loss-level $\Delta$PPL. All diagnostics requested
by \#707C-C3 (granular/clipping MSE, SQNR, hidden-state drift, output KL) are
reported. The robust loss-level signal is FFN$>$attention; the specific top FFN
sub-layer is operating-point/architecture-dependent ($W_{fc2}$ at the paper's
max-clip point, $W_{fc1}$ on Qwen2's gated MLP), explaining the model dependence
noted in \#707C-W2.
